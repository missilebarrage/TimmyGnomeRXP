# TimmyGnome

A [RestedXP Guides](https://github.com/RestedXP/RXPGuides) guide for World of Warcraft: Forever. It's a Gnome
Mage route through Coldridge Valley, Dun Morogh, Elwynn Forest and Loch Modan, levels 1 to 14, made for playing
as a duo.

## Install

You need RestedXP Guides installed.

1. Download [TimmyGnome.zip](https://github.com/missilebarrage/TimmyGnomeRXP/releases/latest/download/TimmyGnome.zip).
2. Extract it into `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`. You should end up with
   `AddOns\TimmyGnome\TimmyGnome.toc`.
3. Restart the game and make sure TimmyGnome is enabled in the AddOns list.
4. In RestedXP's guide menu, pick **TimmyGnome → 1-5 Coldridge Valley**. It moves on to
   **5-10 Dun Morogh**, **10-12 Elwynn Forest** and **12-14 Loch Modan** by itself.

To update, download the zip again and replace the TimmyGnome folder.

## The route

### 1-10 Coldridge Valley and Dun Morogh

- **Levels 1-2:** grind wolves and troggs to level 2 (no Dwarven Outfitters, so no mail deliveries either),
  buy 5 water from Adlin Pridedrift, visit your trainer in Anvilmar, then the Coldridge Valley quests.
- **Level 5:** from the tunnel, discover Chill Breeze Valley, then go to Brewnall for Bitter Rivals and
  Frosthowl, killing every boar on the way for meat and ribs. Then die to the wolves on Iceflow Lake and revive in Kharanos.
- **Kharanos and Steelgrill's Depot:** pick up and turn in everything, then Ammo for Rumbleshot. Reach level 6
  (mages death-skip to Kharanos to train), then the Grizzled Den for the 8 manes and Mountaineer Cornelius only.
- **Boar ribs:** keep collecting them through the west. Beer Basted Boar Ribs goes in on the last Kharanos
  visit, after the Brewnall lap. Stocking Jetsteam goes in before Brewnall, because it unlocks Evershine.
- **Then:** Brewnall and Frostmane Hold, Old Icebeard, a death skip back to Kharanos, then Misty Pine Refuge,
  Vagash and Gol'Bolar Quarry.
- **Earthseer Farsen (level 9+):** Farsen's Watch, Nip 'Em in the Bud and Underground Map, ending with
  Old Ironforge Incursion: the Hall of Thanes dungeon quest. If a Rockjaw Ambusher drops an Empty Powder Keg,
  Stolen Blasting Powder goes to Quarrymaster Thesten; its 16-powder follow-up is optional.
- **Level 10, to Stormwind:** get the Thelsamar flight path in Loch Modan (with Honor Students for Dwarves and
  Gnomes) and set your Hearthstone at the Stoutlager Inn, fly to Ironforge, turn in The Reports, then take the
  Deeprun Tram to Stormwind with Monty's rat quest and Me Brother, Nipsy. The Hall of Thanes quests in Ironforge
  are left for when you run the dungeon.
- **Wands (level 8):** back in Kharanos, the paladin gathers a Copper Rod, Copper Ore and Rough Stone while
  the mage goes to Ironforge: learn Enchanting, buy Motes of Magic and Simple Wood, craft a Runed Copper Rod
  and a Novice's Practice Wand, enchant a bracer to 10 Enchanting, train and craft the Lesser Magic Wand (Tilli
  sells a few Lesser Magic Essences), learn Tailoring across the Great Forge (with Coarse Thread and Bleach from
  Poranna Snowbraid next to him), then hearth back. From here a sticky tracks the Tailoring stash: 30 Bolts of
  Linen Cloth, the thread and the bleach.
- **Skipped:** The Perfect Stout, Flintfire's Shipment and the Frosthowl kill (the quest stays open for later).
- **Training:** every class gets a trainer stop in Anvilmar at levels 2 and 4 (with a vendor stop before the
  second). Mages also train with Magis Sparkmantle in Kharanos at levels 6 and 8.

### 10-12 Elwynn Forest

- **Stormwind:** off the tram, Stormpike's Delivery from Grimand Elmore (if he offers it), the mage's Tailoring
  stop, the flight path from Dungar Longdrink, then walk to Goldshire.
- **Tailoring stop (mage):** top up to about 60 Linen Cloth at the Auction House, bolt it all (Tailoring to about
  25), train White Linen Robe, Brown Linen Pants and Heavy Linen Gloves at the tailoring shop, buy Coarse Thread and
  Bleach, then craft and equip the three. Spare bolts go into a Linen Bag and extra pants for skill points (if the
  Lesser Magic Wand didn't happen in Ironforge, disenchanting a pair gives the essence for it).
- **Goldshire:** train and pick up the Marshal Dughan quests.
- **South:** explore Fargodeep Mine, then the Young Lovers deliveries and Princess Must Die! at the Stonefield
  farm. Back in Goldshire, The Jasperlode Mine (and Elmore's Task, if Grimand wanted it first).
- **East:** explore Jasperlode on the way to Guard Thomas, then the lost guards, Protect the Frontier, Downstream,
  Croaky (level 11 elite) and Princess. Turn in at the bridge and Ridgepoint Tower.
- **Redridge:** Encroaching Gnolls (needs level 11) and the Lakeshire flight path, then fly to Stormwind and walk
  back to Goldshire. Your Hearthstone stays bound in Thelsamar.
- **Finish:** turn-ins in Goldshire and at the farms, Westbrook Garrison, then Westfall: Westfall Stew on the way
  down the road, Report to Gryan Stoutmantle and the Sentinel Hill flight path, and hearth to Thelsamar. The
  Loch Modan guide (still to come) starts there.
- **Level 12 is a target, not a requirement.** Hogger, Riverpaw Gnoll Bounty and grinding are cut because Loch
  Modan pays better by then. With the optional quests you should finish around 85-90% into 11; Return to Brock
  and Stormpike's Delivery (1,050 XP each) wait in Loch Modan.
- **Gathering quests:** left out, except Collecting Kelp (it unlocks The Escape) and Bounty on Murlocs, whose
  items drop from mobs the route already kills. Their accept steps are marked OPTIONAL. Downstream is a required
  step: its three riverbank items respawn instantly. A Bundle of Trouble is skipped on purpose.
- **Paladins:** keep 10 Linen Cloth for The Tome of Divinity, the level 12 class quest.

### 12-14 Loch Modan

- **Thelsamar** (you hearth in): Return to Brock, Snowbound, Mountaineer Stormpike's Task, and a vendor stop.
  Thelsamar Blood Sausages and Rat Catching are OPTIONAL gathering quests for the trip back from Algaz Station.
- **Ironforge at 12:** fly in to train. Paladins start The Tome of Divinity here (John Turner takes the 10 Linen
  Cloth). Below level 12 the trip skips itself and training happens in Ironforge at the end.
- **Southern guard tower:** In Defense of the King's Lands four times over, plus The Trogg Threat on the same troggs.
  The third and fourth (level 15-17 troggs and Grawmug) are OPTIONAL.
- **Dun Morogh detour:** The Lost Pilot and Mangeclaw. Paladins raise Narm Faulk and take the Dark Iron Script.
- **Snowbound:** fill the jar just outside the South Gate Pass, then 10 minutes to Norric Lochthane at the dam.
  Paladins can buy a mace from Nillen Andemar there.
- **Algaz Station:** vendor and repair, Stormpike's Delivery and Mountaineer Stormpike's Task. Stormpike's Order
  goes to Furen Longbeard at the Stormwind tram whenever you're next there.
- **East:** Ironband's Excavation (level 13) and Excavation Progress Report, then Daryl's timed hunts at the
  Farstrider Lodge (OPTIONAL), and hearth back.
- **Ironforge to finish:** Report to Ironforge, the paladin's last Tome of Divinity turn-ins, and level 14
  training if you're there.
- **Skipped as too slow with a launch-day crowd:** Filthy Paws, Bingles' Missing Supplies, Crocolisk Hunting,
  Gathering Idols and Silver of the Waves.
- **Where you end up:** about level 13 with only the core quests, close to 14 with every optional one.
