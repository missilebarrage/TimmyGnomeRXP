# TimmyGnome

A [RestedXP Guides](https://github.com/RestedXP/RXPGuides) guide for World of Warcraft: Forever. It's a Gnome
Mage route through Coldridge Valley, Dun Morogh, Elwynn Forest, Loch Modan, the Hall of Thanes and Darkshore,
levels 1 to about 18, made for playing as a duo (the dungeon needs three more players). From the Hall of Thanes
on, guides are named by the level you start them at, like **14+**: where a duo ends up is hard to predict.

## Install

You need RestedXP Guides installed.

1. Download [TimmyGnome.zip](https://github.com/missilebarrage/TimmyGnomeRXP/releases/latest/download/TimmyGnome.zip).
2. Extract it into `World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns`. You should end up with
   `AddOns\TimmyGnome\TimmyGnome.toc`.
3. Restart the game and make sure TimmyGnome is enabled in the AddOns list.
4. In RestedXP's guide menu, pick **TimmyGnome → 1-5 Coldridge Valley**. It moves on to
   **5-10 Dun Morogh**, **10-12 Elwynn Forest**, **12-14 Loch Modan**, **14+ Hall of Thanes** and
   **15+ Darkshore** by itself.

To update, download the zip again and replace the TimmyGnome folder.

## The route

### 1-10 Coldridge Valley and Dun Morogh

- **Levels 1-2:** grind wolves and troggs to level 2 (no Dwarven Outfitters, so no mail deliveries either),
  buy 5 water from Adlin Pridedrift, visit your trainer in Anvilmar, then the Coldridge Valley quests.
- **Level 5:** from the tunnel, discover Chill Breeze Valley, then go to Brewnall for Bitter Rivals and
  Frosthowl, killing every boar on the way for meat and ribs. Then die to the wolves on Iceflow Lake and revive in Kharanos.
- **Kharanos and Steelgrill's Depot:** pick up and turn in everything, then Ammo for Rumbleshot. Reach level 6
  (mages death-skip to Kharanos to train), then the Grizzled Den for the 8 manes and Mountaineer Cornelius only.
- **Boar ribs:** keep collecting them through the west. Beer Basted Boar Ribs goes in on the last Kharanos
  visit, after the Brewnall lap. Stocking Jetsteam goes in before Brewnall, because it unlocks Evershine.
- **Then:** Brewnall and Frostmane Hold, Old Icebeard, a death skip back to Kharanos, then Misty Pine Refuge,
  Vagash and Gol'Bolar Quarry.
- **Earthseer Farsen (level 9+):** Farsen's Watch, Nip 'Em in the Bud and Underground Map, ending with
  Old Ironforge Incursion: the Hall of Thanes dungeon quest. If a Rockjaw Ambusher drops an Empty Powder Keg,
  Stolen Blasting Powder goes to Quarrymaster Thesten; its 16-powder follow-up is optional.
- **Level 10, to Stormwind:** get the Thelsamar flight path in Loch Modan (with Honor Students for Dwarves and
  Gnomes) and set your Hearthstone at the Stoutlager Inn, fly to Ironforge, learn First Aid from Nissa Firestone
  next to the gryphon master, turn in The Reports, then take the
  Deeprun Tram to Stormwind with Monty's rat quest and Me Brother, Nipsy. The Hall of Thanes quests in Ironforge
  are left for when you run the dungeon.
- **Wands (level 8):** back in Kharanos, the paladin crafts a Copper Rod for the mage and trades him his spare
  Linen Cloth (keeping 10 for The Tome of Divinity). The mage goes to Ironforge: learn Enchanting and take Camping
  101: Enchanting from Thonys Pillarstone next to the trainer, buy Motes of Magic and Simple Wood, craft a Runed
  Copper Rod and a Novice's Practice Wand, enchant a bracer to 10 Enchanting and train the Lesser Magic Wand. Then
  learn Tailoring across the Great Forge (Coarse Thread and Bleach from Poranna Snowbraid next to him), bolt linen
  until Uthrar teaches Brown Linen Pants, and craft and disenchant pants until one gives a Lesser Magic Essence for
  the wand (Tilli and the Auction House sell the essence if the linen runs out). Then hearth back. From here a
  sticky tracks the Tailoring stash: 36 Bolts of Linen Cloth, the thread and the bleach. The paladin's sticky at
  the same time: keep 10 Linen Cloth for his level 12 class quest and trade the rest to the mage.
- **Skipped:** The Perfect Stout, Flintfire's Shipment and the Frosthowl kill (the quest stays open for later).
- **Training:** every class gets a trainer stop in Anvilmar at levels 2 and 4 (with a vendor stop before the
  second). Mages also train with Magis Sparkmantle in Kharanos at levels 6 and 8.

### 10-12 Elwynn Forest

- **Stormwind:** off the tram, Stormpike's Delivery from Grimand Elmore (if he offers it), First Aid from Shaina
  Fuller in the Cathedral of Light (only if you missed it in Ironforge), Fishing from Arnold Leland, the mage's
  Enchanting and Tailoring stop, then three new Forever quests: Fishin' Time (a Shiny Bauble and 3 Nightcrawlers
  from Catherine Leland, handed to Gilbert Gray at Stormwind Harbor), Philmor's Favor (Manifest Clerk Philmor at
  the harbor, delivered to Trias' Cheese) and Gatehouse Goods (Elaine Trias, if she offers it). Then the flight
  path from Dungar Longdrink next door, and the walk to Goldshire.
- **Enchanting (mage):** on the way to the tailoring shop, buy 9 Motes of Magic from Jessara Cordell in the Mage
  Quarter and enchant your bracer (Inferior Stamina) to 20 Enchanting. That completes Camping 101: Enchanting,
  which Thonys Pillarstone in Ironforge takes on the Ironforge stop at the end of Loch Modan and teaches the
  Enchanted Lute camp item.
- **Tailoring stop (mage):** top up to about 72 Linen Cloth at the Auction House, bolt it all (Tailoring to about
  25), train White Linen Robe, Brown Linen Pants and Heavy Linen Gloves at the tailoring shop, buy Pattern: Linen
  Reagent Bag from Antonio Bolero in the same shop, buy Coarse Thread and Bleach, then craft and equip the three
  plus a Linen Reagent Bag (6 slots; make your party one each if you have the bolts and silver). Optional
  nice-to-have: a Reinforced Linen Cape (Spell Power) if your skill
  allows. Spare bolts go into a Linen Bag and extra pants for skill points (if the Lesser Magic Wand didn't happen
  in Ironforge, disenchanting a pair gives the essence for it).
- **Goldshire:** train and pick up the Marshal Dughan quests. The mage also takes Speak with Jennea from Zaldimar
  Wefhellt after training.
- **South:** explore Fargodeep Mine, then the Young Lovers deliveries and Princess Must Die! at the Stonefield
  farm. Back in Goldshire, The Jasperlode Mine (and Elmore's Task, if Grimand wanted it first).
- **East:** explore Jasperlode on the way to Guard Thomas, then the lost guards, Protect the Frontier, Downstream,
  Croaky (level 11 elite) and Princess. Turn in at the bridge and Ridgepoint Tower. OPTIONAL on the way: Delicate
  Instruments from the Tower of Azora (Kobold Geomancers at Jasperlode, then Defias Rogue Wizards on the way to
  Rolf) and Red Linen Goods (bandanas from those Rogue Wizards and the Defias at Princess). With Delicate
  Instruments the mage also takes An Enchanting Lesson (enchanters only, 480 XP) from Kitta Firewind at the top of
  the tower: the Geomancers drop Crude Wax Effigies, which disenchant into the 3 Luminous Residue it wants. No
  farming: if the kills don't give 3, the mage drops it. The paladin's sticky says to let the mage have the
  effigies.
- **Redridge:** Encroaching Gnolls (needs level 11) and the Lakeshire flight path, then fly to Stormwind and walk
  back to Goldshire. Your Hearthstone stays bound in Thelsamar. In Stormwind the mage hands Speak with Jennea to
  Jennea Cannon in the Wizard's Sanctum, takes Mirror Lake and, at level 12, trains with her (the Mirror Lake
  turn-in is a second chance).
- **Finish:** turn-ins in Goldshire and at the farms, Applejack Still and An Apple Treat (4 apples from Homer
  Stonefield into the still by the Mirror Lake falls, a repeatable hand-in with no XP, then the Thunder Applejack to
  Sergeant De Vries for 625 XP), Westbrook Garrison, then Westfall: Westfall Stew on the way down the road, Report
  to Gryan Stoutmantle and the Sentinel Hill flight path, and hearth to Thelsamar. The Loch Modan guide starts
  there. The mage fills Jennea's Flask at the Mirror Lake waterfall on the way to the still, then flies from
  Sentinel Hill to Stormwind before hearthing. Meanwhile the paladin (a Dwarf, once he's 12) flies to Ironforge to
  start The Tome of Divinity: level 12 training with Brandur Ironhammer, then Tiza Battleforge, John Turner (the 10
  Linen Cloth) and Muiredon, then he hearths and you meet up in Thelsamar. At Sentinel Hill the mage also buys
  Pattern: Red Linen Bag from Gina MacGregor (learn it at 50 Tailoring). In Stormwind: 2 Minor Mana
  Potions from the Auction House, Novice Arcanist's Sash (needs 35 Tailoring; the potions are its reagent) and
  Green Linen Bracers from the tailoring shop, crafted and equipped on the spot, then Mirror Lake (Ley Staff or
  Ley Orb) at Jennea. A sticky from the first Stormwind stop reminds the mage to keep linen for these.
- **Level 12 is a target, not a requirement.** Hogger, Riverpaw Gnoll Bounty and grinding are cut because Loch
  Modan pays better by then. In a beta test run the mage was already 12 at Sergeant De Vries, before Westfall.
  Return to Brock and Stormpike's Delivery (1,050 XP each) wait in Loch Modan.
- **Gathering quests:** left out, except Red Linen Goods, whose bandanas come from Defias on the route. Its accept
  step is marked OPTIONAL. Downstream is a required step: its three riverbank items respawn instantly. Skipped on
  purpose: Collecting Kelp (and with it The Escape) and Bottles and Baubles (Crystal Lake's murlocs and junk piles
  won't hold up on launch day), A Bundle of Trouble, Stolen Enchanting Supplies (crates in gnoll camps off the
  route), Hogger (his turn-in is in Goldshire, behind you by then) and the Goldtooth chain (a single spawn). Guard
  Thomas doesn't offer Bounty on Murlocs in Forever.
- **Paladins:** keep 10 Linen Cloth for The Tome of Divinity, the level 12 class quest you start in Ironforge at
  the end of Elwynn.

### 12-14 Loch Modan

- **Thelsamar** (you hearth in): Return to Brock, Snowbound, Mountaineer Stormpike's Task, and a vendor stop.
  Rat Catching is OPTIONAL (its kobolds are on the trip back from Algaz Station).
- **Southern guard tower:** In Defense of the King's Lands four times over, plus The Trogg Threat on the same troggs.
  The third and fourth (level 15-17 troggs and Grawmug) are OPTIONAL.
- **Dun Morogh detour:** The Lost Pilot and Mangeclaw. Paladins raise Narm Faulk and take the Dark Iron Script.
- **Snowbound:** fill the jar just outside the South Gate Pass, then 10 minutes to Norric Lochthane at the dam.
  Paladins can buy a mace from Nillen Andemar there.
- **Algaz Station:** vendor and repair, Stormpike's Delivery and Mountaineer Stormpike's Task. Stormpike's Order
  goes to Furen Longbeard at the Stormwind tram whenever you're next there.
- **East:** Ironband's Excavation (level 13) and Excavation Progress Report, then Daryl's timed hunts at the
  Farstrider Lodge (OPTIONAL), and hearth back.
- **Ironforge to finish:** Report to Ironforge, the paladin's last Tome of Divinity turn-ins, the mage's Camping
  101: Enchanting at Thonys Pillarstone, and level 14 training if you're there. Anyone who missed level 12
  training (the mage with Jennea, the paladin in Ironforge at the end of Elwynn) gets it here. Last, the mage
  trains Linen Belt from Uthrar Threx and crafts belts to 50 Tailoring (15 from 35, the cheapest way).
- **Skipped as too slow with a launch-day crowd:** Filthy Paws, Bingles' Missing Supplies, Crocolisk Hunting,
  Gathering Idols, Silver of the Waves and Thelsamar Blood Sausages.
- **Where you end up:** about level 13 with only the core quests, close to 14 with every optional one.

### 14+ Hall of Thanes

- **A dungeon:** the Hall of Thanes is a 5-player dungeon under Ironforge, so find three more players (there's no
  group finder or summoning stone in Forever). Its quests pay a dungeon bonus, 2.2 times the usual XP: about
  13,300 for the five, a level at 14-15.
- **Way in:** King Magni's throne room, the spiderweb corridor on the left, then the stairs down to Old
  Ironforge. The Restless Dead (Afadra Dunwall) and Important Heirlooms (Thom Filch) are on the way. Old Ironforge
  Incursion is Earthseer Farsen's quest from Dun Morogh; without it, its steps skip themselves.
- **Inside:** An Ancient Grudge from the Ghostly Attendant and Faldrim Anvilmar, then Magmatus, Plunder and
  Durgen Dirgehammer, with boss tips and the loot worth rolling on. Stickies track the souls and the heirlooms,
  and each gets a real finish step. The heirlooms and Durgen's head are per player. The Treaty of Understanding
  is a tablet behind a vault door in Durgen's room.
- **Turn-ins:** the Ghostly Attendant (right after Faldrim, or on the way out), Thom Filch, Afadra and King
  Magni. The mage takes Tomb Robber's Gloves, the Dusty Belt and Deepblaze (an 18.5 DPS wand); the paladin takes
  the Cryptwalker Bracers and the Ironforge Greathammer.
- **Training:** level 16 here only if you're already 16; there's no mage or paladin trainer in Darkshore.

### 15+ Darkshore

- **Getting there:** the tram to Stormwind (Stormpike's Order goes in at Furen Longbeard), then the boat from
  Stormwind Harbor's southernmost dock to Auberdine. The Hearthstone goes to Auberdine.
- **The route:** RestedXP's Forever Darkshore route for a non-Night Elf, with nearly every quest that's quick or
  pays well, in six loops out of Auberdine:
  - **South:** Buzzbox 827's crawler legs, Washed Ashore, the bear trap and the furbolg camp.
  - **North:** thresher eyes, the two sunken lockboxes, Bashal'Aran and the red crystal.
  - **Ameth'Aran:** its two tablets, the Ancient Flame and the Blackwood furbolgs.
  - **North-east:** the crystal again, the Cliffspring River, the moonstalker fangs and the cave mushrooms.
  - **Far south:** the rest of the bears, Onu's Master's Glaive chain, the Twilight camp, the Grizzled Thistle
    Bears' scalps, Remtravel, Murkdeep and the beached creatures.
  - **Far north-east:** Den Mother, the Blackwood stores and Xabraxxis, the Tower of Althalaxx and Gyromast,
    then a hearth back.
- **Drops:** every drop quest has a sticky plus a real finish step you can skip. OPTIONAL: the Buzzbox chain
  (four drop quests, about 4,100 XP, and its crawler legs drop rarely), For Love Eternal (Anaya is a rare),
  WANTED: Jai'vhanel, Bashal'Aran's satyr seal and Gyromast's Revenge.
- **Left out:** Easy Strider Living (Cooking 10), The Family and the Fishing Pole (Auction House fish), Tools of
  the Highborne, the Remtravel escort, and quests that end outside Darkshore: Therylune's Escape, The Tower of
  Althalaxx's third part, Mathystra Relics, A Lost Master's second part and The Powers Below.
- **Where you end up:** about 18.5-19 for a duo doing nearly everything (about 17.5 if the Hall of Thanes bonus
  doesn't apply and drops go badly). Level 16 and 18 training waits for your next city.
